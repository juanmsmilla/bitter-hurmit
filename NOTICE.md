## Modifications

- The lowercase `l` glyph was replaced with a metric-adjusted copy of
  Liberation Mono's `l` (scaled to the ascender, stem weight and cell width,
  and sheared to the italic angle for the italic faces).
- The family was renamed to **Hurmit Nerd Font Custom** so it does not
  collide with the packaged Hurmit Nerd Font and does not use a reserved
  font name.
- All other glyphs and metrics are unchanged.
